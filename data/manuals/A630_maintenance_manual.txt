Machine Type: Compressed Air Station
Title: Compressed Air Station Maintenance Manual

Error Code: E-6309
Description: Delivered air pressure has dropped below the plant service threshold.

Possible Causes:
- Leak in a distribution branch
- Clogged intake filtration
- Pressure sensor malfunction

Recommended Procedure:
1. Notify facilities maintenance and affected production areas.
2. Record the alarm and service-pressure readings.
3. Do not open pressurised components; use approved isolation procedures.
4. Have authorised personnel investigate leaks, filtration and instrumentation.

Safety Note:
Only authorised staff may inspect or service equipment. Follow the site's approved safety, isolation and return-to-service procedures. This is synthetic training data, not operational guidance.
