Document ID: MAN-C105-001
Machine: C-105
Machine Type: Conveyor Belt System
Title: Conveyor Maintenance Manual

Error Code: E-1052
Description: Conveyor motor temperature exceeds the operating threshold.

Possible Causes:
- Excessive mechanical load
- Worn motor bearings
- Insufficient ventilation

Recommended Procedure:
1. Notify the maintenance team.
2. Stop the conveyor using the approved procedure.
3. Inspect for excessive loading.
4. Have authorised personnel inspect the motor and bearings.
5. Record findings in the maintenance log.
