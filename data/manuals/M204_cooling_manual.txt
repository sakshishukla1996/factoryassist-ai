Document ID: MAN-M204-001
Machine: M-204
Machine Type: Industrial Cooling Unit
Title: Cooling System Maintenance Manual

Error Code: E-2047
Description: Cooling temperature exceeds the normal operating range.

Possible Causes:
- Blocked cooling filter
- Reduced coolant circulation
- Faulty temperature sensor

Recommended Procedure:
1. Notify the maintenance technician.
2. Follow the approved shutdown and isolation procedure.
3. Inspect the cooling filter for blockage.
4. Check coolant circulation according to the maintenance instructions.
5. Escalate to the maintenance supervisor if the issue persists.

Safety Note:
Only authorised personnel may inspect or service the equipment.
