Document ID: MAN-P301-001
Machine: P-301
Machine Type: Hydraulic Transfer Pump
Title: Hydraulic Transfer Pump Maintenance Manual

Error Code: E-3018
Description: Discharge pressure is below the configured operating window.

Possible Causes:
- Restricted suction strainer
- Air entering through an inlet seal
- Incorrect pressure transducer reading

Recommended Procedure:
1. Notify the shift technician and log the low-pressure alarm.
2. Follow the site-approved isolation and inspection workflow.
3. Have authorised personnel review the strainer, inlet seal and pressure sensor records.
4. Escalate if the alarm remains after verification.

Safety Note:
Only authorised staff may inspect or service equipment. Follow the site's approved safety, isolation and return-to-service procedures. This is synthetic training data, not operational guidance.
