Machine: S-740
Machine Type: Packaging Heat Sealer
Title: Packaging Heat Sealer Maintenance Manual

Error Code: E-7402
Description: Seal-jaw temperature is below the validated packaging setpoint.

Possible Causes:
- Heater cartridge degradation
- Temperature controller drift
- Premature restart after warm-up

Recommended Procedure:
1. Stop release of potentially affected packaging lots.
2. Notify packaging maintenance and quality assurance.
3. Follow approved safe access controls before inspection.
4. Revalidate seal quality before restarting production.

Safety Note:
Only authorised staff may inspect or service equipment. Follow the site's approved safety, isolation and return-to-service procedures. This is synthetic training data, not operational guidance.
